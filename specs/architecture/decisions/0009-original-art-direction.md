# 0009 — Original/Licensed Art, Not Square Enix IP

## Status
Accepted

## Context
The product vision describes the aesthetic as "similar to Final Fantasy 1's overworld." That's a style reference, not permission to use Square Enix's actual sprites, tiles, or other copyrighted assets.

## Decision
All shipped art (character sprites, portraits, tiles) must be original work, commissioned work, or properly licensed (e.g. a royalty-free asset pack) in a similar pixel-art style — never extracted or derived from actual Final Fantasy 1 assets.

## Consequences
- Avoids IP infringement risk entirely.
- Adds asset-sourcing/creation as real project work (budget or time), rather than assuming placeholder assets can ship as final art.
- Worth deciding art direction/source early since it affects timeline — see [product/overview.md](../../product/overview.md#constraints).
